HxH Bot — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use HxH Bot, we may store information associated with your Discord account and your use of the Bot.

This may include:

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord username
  • In-game currency
  • Character and card collections
  • Pulls and progression
  • Teams and game statistics
  • Trading information
  • Other information necessary for the Bot's features

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and maintain the Bot's features, including game profiles, progression, trading, commands, and other gameplay systems.

Information may also be used to prevent abuse, investigate exploits, maintain security, and improve the Bot.

3. Data Storage

Game data may be stored in a database operated by the Bot's developers or their service providers.

Access to stored data is limited to the Bot's developers and authorized personnel when reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, or secure the Bot.

4. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally sell your personal information.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to operate the Bot, protect the Bot and its users, investigate security issues, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

5. Discord

HxH Bot operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

HxH Bot is not operated by Discord.

6. Data Retention

Game data may remain stored while your account is active or while it is reasonably necessary for the Bot's operation.

Inactive or deleted data may be removed according to the Bot's data-retention practices.

7. Data Deletion Requests

If you want to request deletion of eligible data associated with your Bot account, contact the Bot's development team through the official Support Server .

Some information may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal, or technical reasons.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information. However, no online service or database can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Changes will be reflected on this page by updating the "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Bot's development team through the official Support Server .
